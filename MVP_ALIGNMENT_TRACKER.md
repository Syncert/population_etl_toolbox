## MVP Completion

The architecture MVP and the Analytics-design MVP surface are complete and live-verified. The external stack passes API, geometry, tile, cache, route, and headless-browser checks.

Post-MVP work from the broader manifesto remains intentionally phased: server-side saved-chart/article persistence, user accounts, public publishing workflows, source-native BLS/FRED explorers, additional transformations and chart types, collaboration, and advanced analytics.

## Steering Rule

Work through the list in order unless a later step blocks an earlier one. Verification counts as part of each step, not a separate afterthought.
