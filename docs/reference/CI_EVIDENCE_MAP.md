Branch protection should require the fourteen PR/push jobs above by their displayed job names. `frontend-smoke` joined that list on 2026-09-12: the workflow had existed since the live-stack tier was built and `TESTING_CONTRACT.md` named it as the owner of WEB-027 and WEB-033, but neither this register nor its executable mirror knew it, so deleting the workflow or renaming its job would have failed no test. A release candidate additionally records successful live-provider and bounded E2E runs no older than seven days. The million-row profile remains opt-in and is required only when a release changes bulk loading, chunking, or serving-query plans.

The executable mirror is `tests/support/ci_evidence_manifest.json`; unit validation fails when a named workflow/job disappears or an architecture-sensitive owning path loses its trigger.
