## Short answer

Each year the Population Estimates Program publishes a new vintage: a complete series of estimates from the last census to the most recent July 1. Vintage 2025 contains estimates for 2020 through 2025, and it replaces the 2020 through 2024 values that Vintage 2024 published.

A series read from one vintage is consistent with itself. Values from two vintages were made with different inputs.

## What it is not

- It is not the year an estimate describes. A Vintage 2025 estimate can be for July 1, 2021.
- Joining the newest year of each vintage into one line is not a time series. The changes between those points mix real change with revisions.

## Worked example

If Vintage 2024 put a county at 585,000 in 2024 and Vintage 2025 puts it at 583,000 for 2024 and 590,000 for 2025, the county grew by 7,000 between those years, not 5,000.

## Where it is used

The People and Change chapters of place pages, which read one vintage at a time.
